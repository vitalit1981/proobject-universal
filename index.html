The Problem

Construction work often starts with incomplete and fragmented information. Contractors waste hours deciphering vague requests, while clients don't know what is actually included in the price. Lead marketplaces provide contacts but don't solve the core issues: missing data, site conditions, risk allocation, and price justification. This leads to disputes, delays, cost overruns, and failed deals.

The Solution

PROobject is an AI-assisted platform that structures incoming requests into a decision-ready package. It doesn't replace professional engineers — it reduces initial uncertainty and creates a common language for both sides.

Input

Text, photos, drawings, BOQs, defect lists, spreadsheets

Output

Object passport, clarification questions, risk signals, preliminary estimate, proposal draft

How It Works

  • User role and purpose are defined
  • Source materials are uploaded
  • Object passport is generated
  • Missing data and site factors are detected
  • Clarification questions are prepared
  • Scope, team, schedule, risks and price are updated
  • Proposal, contract draft, or next workflow artifact is generated

Blockchain Layer for Document Verification (NEAR)

We are building a verification module that will run on the NEAR Protocol testnet. The module allows construction professionals to create a cryptographic fingerprint (hash) of key project documents (designs, estimates, acceptance protocols) and record it on the blockchain.

How AI Agents Work Inside the Module

  • Recognition & Structuring — AI agents read information from uploaded documents (photos, PDFs, drawings, tables, scans) and convert unstructured data into a unified format: scope of work, materials, labor, timelines, conditions.
  • Key Parameter Extraction — agents automatically extract all significant technical and economic indicators from documents: pipe diameters, section lengths, installation methods, testing requirements, special conditions.
  • Cross-Referencing with Real Conditions — based on the extracted information, AI agents generate an object passport, identify missing data, highlight risks, and prepare clarifying questions for the client.
  • Blockchain Recording — after the data is structured and verified, the system creates a hash of the final parameter set and records it on NEAR. This locks in what was agreed at each stage and allows both sides to refer to an immutable record in case of disputes.

Product Uniqueness — Training on Real 20‑Year Experience

Unlike typical AI solutions trained on generic data, PROobject agents are trained on a unique knowledge base built on the founder's 20 years of hands‑on experience in design, construction, and project handover.

What this delivers:

  • Real‑world hidden pitfalls — the system knows not just theory, but what engineers, foremen, and estimators actually face: hidden works, confined sites, impossibility of shutdowns, seasonal constraints, crew mobilisation, Rostechnadzor documentation, heat network requirements, and much more.
  • Justified risks — agents don't just list possible issues; they highlight the risks that actually affect timelines and costs, based on thousands of real‑life cases.
  • Practical clarifying questions — the system asks not abstract questions, but the ones an experienced engineer would ask upon first site review.
  • Realistic estimates — preliminary calculations are not based on average standards, but on an understanding of actual labor intensity and cost under specific conditions.

What We Are Building Specifically for NEAR

  • AI agents with unique industry logic — the platform turns disparate files and requests into a unified structured object database, drawing on accumulated engineering expertise.
  • Document fingerprinting — proof that a document existed at a specific point in time.
  • Milestone verification — recording and verifying project stage statuses and approvals.
  • Data privacy — only document hashes and key parameters are stored on‑chain. Full documents and calculations remain with users, entirely private.
  • Open source — the module and AI agent logic will be published as open source, in line with NEAR grant requirements.

Why This Matters for Clients and Contractors

  • For the client — a structured, verified description of what they are procuring, with blockchain‑backed records, and confidence that all real‑world conditions have been considered.
  • For the contractor — a clear scope and terms, elimination of ambiguity during execution, and well‑justified risks and timelines.
  • In case of disputes — an immutable record of what was agreed, plus an AI agent that can quickly provide a comparative analysis of "what was in the documents" vs. "what was actually delivered", based on practical scenarios.
