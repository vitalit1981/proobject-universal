PROobject

AI‑powered construction workflow — from a messy request to a clear deal foundation

We help contractors and customers build a shared technical and economic basis for construction projects. Our platform turns photos, drawings, BOQs, defect lists and text requests into an object passport, identifies missing data, highlights risks, and generates a preliminary scope, crew, schedule and price range.

Problem

Construction work often starts with incomplete and fragmented information. Contractors waste hours deciphering vague requests, while customers don't know what is really included in the price. Lead marketplaces provide contacts, but they do not resolve the uncertainty that follows: missing data, site conditions, risk allocation, and price justification. This leads to disputes, delays, cost overruns, and failed deals.

Solution

PROobject is an AI‑assisted workflow platform that structures incoming requests into a decision-ready package. It does not replace professional engineers, but it narrows the initial guesswork and creates a common language for both sides.

Input

Text, photos, drawings, BOQs, defect reports, spreadsheets

Output

Object passport, clarification questions, risk cues, preliminary estimate, proposal draft

Workflow

  • User role and purpose defined
  • Source materials uploaded
  • Object passport generated
  • Missing data and site factors detected
  • Clarification questions prepared
  • Updated scope, team, schedule, risks and price range
  • Proposal, contract-support document or next workflow artifact

Blockchain layer (ecosystem-agnostic)

We are building a public‑safe proof module that demonstrates how a construction document fingerprint and milestone status can be recorded on a public testnet. We are actively exploring NEAR and Algorand as potential ecosystems, and we remain open to other networks that offer strong grant programs and technical fit for our use case.

  • Document fingerprinting – cryptographically verify that a document existed at a specific time
  • Milestone verification – record status changes and approvals in a tamper‑evident way
  • No real funds or tokens – all data is synthetic and no escrow is claimed in the MVP
  • Open source – the proof module will be published as open source, aligning with grant requirements
